Apocalypse 101
Survival RPG

After everyone mysteriously disappears, a teacher and a classroom of students must work together to figure out what happened and not die in the process.

A party-based survival RPG where players lead a group of students through an abandoned world, scavenging resources, fortifying the classroom, developing skills, and making difficult decisions as they struggle to survive and uncover the mystery behind humanity's disappearance.

POV: Primary: Top-down a la Undertale and Dragon Quest V; Secondary: Fisrt person for Combat, Special NPC Interaction, Special obstacles.
Controls: Mouse/KB and Game Controller

Exploration
Player controls the player character with cardinal directions. Party Members aut follow. The group's movement is determined by the composition of the members. For example, if all of the member are able to jump over an obstacle excpet for one, the group is not able to clear the obstacle (no one left behind)

Collection
Party can collect items for stat recovery and buff. Items for crafting equipment. Clue that inform the direction of play and narrative context.

Crafting
Certain Items can be crafted on carfting tables. There is a crafting table in the classroom and strwen about on campus. There is a character ability that allows the group to craft anywhere. Cafting unlocks new gear and skills.

Combat
Combat is turn-based, first-person (a la Don't Look Outide). Player selects target for each party member. Attacks can carry a skill-based component that affectas outcome (e.g. memory, button mashing, timing, aiming). Most attacks a damage dealing, but there are some that have status affects on the target and also permit healing and buffing.
